These are the workshop projects that are out in the open: the Tomorrow Gazette archive, a journey through cosmic time, and two music tools you can use in the browser. Two more visual pieces are still on the bench.

    The Unfolding

    One particle body travels 13.8 billion years, from the first instant to a dying star’s nebula. Every stage carries an evidence label and its sources.

  • Music

    MelodyFlow

    A songbook and practice tool for a steel tongue drum and a ukulele. It works offline, asks for no account, and tracks nobody.

    Open MelodyFlow Source

  • Music

    Refrain

    Public-domain words, set to music, checked by a person, then played with the lyrics following the audio. Nothing is generated while you listen.
